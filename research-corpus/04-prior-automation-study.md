# Prior Automation Study

**STATUS: REVIEWED FINDING · OLDER STUDY · CURRENTNESS NOT RECHECKED**

**Study date:** January 2026

**Product area:** Rule Builder, a different Cyberdyne automation feature.

**Participants:** 10 experienced enterprise administrators who used automation regularly.

## Reviewed finding

Experienced administrators valued automation most when they could predict when the system would act and when they could intervene.

## What it may help with

It provides adjacent context about predictability and control in automation for experienced administrators.

## Scope note

This was a different feature, with experienced administrators, studied in January 2026. Treat it as adjacent context rather than direct evidence about first-use Auto-Actions.
