# Auto-Actions Study Brief

**STATUS: STUDY BRIEF · CURRENT · SEPTEMBER 2026**

## What we studied

How current Cyberdyne enterprise users understand Auto-Actions during first use, especially what they think will happen before an automated action is carried out.

## Participants

8 current Cyberdyne enterprise users who had not used Auto-Actions before.

## Method

60-minute remote prototype sessions. Participants were shown the setup flow, asked to explain what they expected the system to do, and then completed first-use tasks.

## What this study can help us understand

- First-use comprehension of Auto-Actions
- Where people hesitate or misunderstand the confirmation step
- Expectations about when the system will act

## Study scope

This study focused on first-use comprehension during setup. It did not test long-term use, measure time savings in real work, or evaluate security, privacy, or compliance requirements.

The sessions were designed around first-use expectations and setup behavior.
