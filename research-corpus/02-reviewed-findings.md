# Reviewed Findings

**STATUS: REVIEWED FINDINGS · CURRENT · SEPTEMBER 2026**

## Finding 1

5 of 8 participants hesitated before enabling Auto-Actions because they were unsure whether an action could happen before they reviewed it.

**Support:** P02, P03, P05, P06, P08

**What it supports:** First-use uncertainty about when Auto-Actions will act.

**Scope note:** This finding comes from first-use setup sessions with 8 current enterprise users.

## Finding 2

Participants who correctly understood that they would see a confirmation step were generally comfortable continuing setup.

**Variation:** Two participants still felt uncomfortable even after understanding the confirmation step.

**What it supports:** Understanding the confirmation step appears related to comfort during setup, but it does not eliminate concern for everyone.

**Scope note:** This relationship was observed during setup. Two participants remained uncomfortable even after understanding the confirmation step.
