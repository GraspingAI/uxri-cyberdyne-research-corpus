# Product Decision Memo

**STATUS: PRODUCT DECISION · NOT RESEARCH EVIDENCE**

## Q4 product direction

Product leadership has decided that reducing setup friction is a Q4 priority.

## Current working direction

The team is exploring whether Auto-Actions should be enabled by default for new enterprise accounts.

## Why leadership is considering it

- Reduce setup steps
- Increase feature discovery
- Make automation feel more immediate

## Open questions

- Whether users understand what happens before an action executes
- Whether different user groups need different levels of confirmation
- Whether security or compliance requirements constrain the rollout

This memo records a product decision and assumptions. It is useful context for research, but it is not evidence about what users think or what the product should do.
